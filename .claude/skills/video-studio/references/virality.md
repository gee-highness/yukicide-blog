# Virality checklist

Nothing guarantees a hit; this raises the odds and makes results measurable. Do it at the brief stage and again before packaging.

## Pick the idea
Score each 0 to 2 in `IDEAS.md`: **Curiosity** (would a stranger click the title?), **Emotion** (awe, outrage, humour, fear, nostalgia), **Share** (would someone send it to a friend, and what would they say?), **Fresh angle** (not the same as the top 10 results), **Feasibility** (sources and visuals exist). Build 8 and above first.

## Packaging
- Title: 4 to 9 words, one open question or surprising claim, a concrete noun, no clickbait the video cannot pay off. Write 10, keep 3.
- Thumbnail: one focal point, up to 4 words, high contrast, emotion or mystery, readable at phone size, different from the title text but completing it.
- First sentence and first frame work with sound off and without context.

## Retention (long form)
- 0 to 30 s: hook, promise, proof there is a payoff. No logo animation, no channel intro.
- Change something every 3 to 6 s (image, camera move, text, sound).
- Re-hook at about 25%, 50% and 75% with a new question or twist.
- Remove anything that does not move the story: cut after the first draft, aim for 10 to 20% shorter.
- End screen: point to one specific next video.

## Retention (shorts, reels, TikTok)
- Hook in 3 s on screen and in voice; loop-able ending; captions; 15 to 45 s unless the story earns more.
- Post several cuts of one story with different hooks; keep the best.
- Pinned comment that asks a debatable question.

## After posting
Log numbers in `feedback.md` after 48 hours and 7 days: views, average view duration, retention graph drop-off second, click-through rate, shares. Where the graph drops, fix that kind of moment next time and add a lesson.
