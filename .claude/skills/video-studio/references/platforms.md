# Platforms and formats

Limits and rules change: re-check each year (values below are from the Venda Khona research, Nov 2026, Southern Africa). Pick platforms in the brief.

| Where | Send | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube (long) | 1920x1080 (or 3840x2160), mp4 H.264 | 6 to 20 min | Watch hours count toward the Partner Program (1,000 subscribers and 4,000 hours, or 10 million Shorts views in 90 days; stricter rules announced from Feb 2027). Title, thumbnail and first 30 s decide retention |
| YouTube Shorts | 1080x1920 | up to 3 min (vertical/square) | Use own, generated or licensed music only |
| TikTok | 1080x1920 | 15 to 60 s for reach | Largest adult reach in South Africa; growing fast in Mozambique |
| Facebook Reels | 1080x1920 | in-app above 90 s | Biggest platform in much of Africa; link in the first comment |
| Instagram Reels | 1080x1920 | 15 to 90 s | Strongest in South Africa and Namibia |
| WhatsApp Status | 720x1280 copy | up to 90 s | First frame must read as a still |
| Posters / community posts | still frame | n/a | QR codes must be real ones |
| Thumbnails | 1280x720, under 2 MB | n/a | Big readable words (max 4), one face or one object, high contrast; must agree with the title |

## Audience
Set in `feedback.md` (channel audience). Earlier research for Southern Africa: Eswatini 513 k social identities, South Africa 29.1 m, Botswana 1.40 m, Namibia 797 k, Lesotho 653 k, Zimbabwe 2.60 m, Zambia 4.10 m, Malawi 2.40 m, Mozambique 4.10 m (Portuguese); median age 18 to 29, so write for young adults.

## Posting rhythm
Consistency beats volume: a fixed weekly slot for long form, a short most days. Best times seen: 06:30 to 07:30, 15:00 to 16:30, 20:30 to 21:45, Sunday 17:00 to 19:00 (local). Give every post a link tag (`<id>-yt`, `<id>-tt`...) so results can be logged in `feedback.md`.
