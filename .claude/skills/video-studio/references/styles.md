# Visual styles

Choose one style per video (or per series) so the channel is recognisable. Add new styles here with when to use them.

| Style | Look | Use for | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Archive documentary | Real or public-domain photos/footage, slow push-ins, film grain, muted colour, serif titles | History, true stories, mysteries | Unlicensed images |
| Illustrated documentary | Generated or drawn illustrations in one consistent style, parallax | Stories with no usable footage | Mixing illustration styles; presenting art as photo |
| Motion graphics explainer | Flat shapes, animated text, maps, charts | Science, economics, how things work | Walls of text |
| Dark cinematic | Dark palette, one accent colour, big type, low music | Mystery, crime, tech, space | Low contrast captions |
| Paper / ink cartoon | SVG drawn cartoon, ink lines, paper grain | Comedy and skits | Dark backgrounds with brand-green text (use light accent) |
| Chalkboard | Chalk lines on a dark board | Teaching, steps, maths | Fine detail |
| Neon | Glow lines on black | Tech, nightlife, futuristic | Long reading |
| Screen-record + voice | Captured screens with highlights | Tutorials | Showing private data |

Rules: every style must keep text readable (contrast, safe margins 90 px, captions between y 1450 and 1670 on vertical), keep one accent colour, and be checked on the first frame as a thumbnail.
